# Stage 1 hand-calculation problems

Rules: try each problem by hand first. Show the free-body diagram, every equation and every unit. Write your answer in `## My answers` below, then check against `stage1-answer-key.md`. If your answer differs, find the step where you differ before looking at anything else.

Material values (E and similar) given in a problem are practice inputs, not data for real design.

## Statics
S1. Simply supported beam, 5 m, pin at A (left), roller at B (right). Downward 20 kN load 2 m from A. Find Ax, Ay, By.
S2. Simply supported beam, 8 m, pin at A, roller at B. Uniform downward load 5 kN/m over the whole length. Find the reactions. (Replace the distributed load by its resultant: size and where it acts.)
S3. Cantilever, 3 m, fixed at A, downward 15 kN at the free end. Find the reactions at A, including the moment, with direction.
S4. Simply supported beam, 10 m, pin at A, roller at B. Downward loads: 10 kN at 3 m from A and 20 kN at 7 m from A. Find Ay and By.
S5. A 2D beam has a pin at both ends and only vertical loads. Count the unknown reactions. Can statics alone find them all? Explain.

## Mechanics of materials
M1. Round rod, diameter 20 mm, axial tension 50 kN. Find the normal stress in MPa.
M2. Rod 2 m long under uniform axial stress 100 MPa, E = 200 GPa (practice value). Find the strain and the elongation in mm.
M3. Solid round shaft, diameter 40 mm, torque 500 N.m. Find the maximum shear stress in MPa. (Use tau = T r / J.)
M4. Rectangular beam section 50 mm wide by 100 mm deep (100 mm vertical), bending moment 5 kN.m about the horizontal axis. Find the maximum bending stress in MPa. (Use sigma = M c / I.)
M5. Bolt, diameter 12 mm, in single shear, force 10 kN. Find the average shear stress in MPa.
